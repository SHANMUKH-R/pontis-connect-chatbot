# About the Riverside Alliance

The Riverside Alliance is a nonprofit council of 14 neighboring towns along the river. It was founded in 2011 so that
small local governments could solve shared problems together, such as recycling, road salt purchasing and flood planning.

## Who are the members

Members are towns, not individuals. Each member town appoints up to two representatives. Residents take part through
their own town council.

## Overview and mission

The mission is to help neighboring towns share services, apply for grants together and speak with one voice.
