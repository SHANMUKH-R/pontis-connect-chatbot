# Membership

## How a town joins

A town joins by passing a council resolution and paying annual dues. Dues are based on the town's population. The dollar
amounts are not published; staff send an invoice after the resolution is received.

## Representatives

Each member town appoints up to two representatives by January 31 each year. Each town has one vote at the Annual Assembly.
