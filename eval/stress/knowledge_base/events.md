# Events

## Annual Assembly

The Annual Assembly is held each October. Representatives vote on the budget and on resolutions.

## Spring Cleanup Day

Spring Cleanup Day is April 12. Volunteers collect litter along the river trail from 9 am to noon.

## Cost and registration

All Alliance events are free. To register, email events@riverside-alliance.example. Staff confirm registrations by email.
